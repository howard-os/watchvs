How It Works

Hybrid Partial Bubble Sort with User-Guided Tiers

The Process

  1. User Input Phase: You select your 5 favorite watches and 5 least favorite watches from the full collection
  2. Tier Setup: Creates 3 tiers:
    • Top 5 (your selected favorites, normalized)
    • Middle 1 (the unselected watch)
    • Bottom 5 (your selected least favorites, normalized)
  3. Limited Bubble Sort: Runs bubble sort but stops after ~22 comparisons:
    • Phase 1: Fully sorts top 5 + middle watch (~10-15 comparisons)
    • Phase 2: Rough sorts bottom 5 (~5-8 comparisons in 1-2 passes)
  4. Results: You get your final ranking with precise ordering at the top

Why This Method?

Full bubble sort requires 55 comparisons for 11 items. This hybrid approach uses only ~22 comparisons—roughly 40% of the full cost.

Key insight: You care much more about your top 5 being ranked accurately than about the exact ordering of your least favorites. By precisely sorting only what matters and rough-sorting the rest, we get fast, meaningful rankings.

Trade-off: Precision in the top tier, speed overall, and a roughly-ordered bottom tier.
